## Statement

At reminder time the macOS app plays a sound once and speaks an explanation of the event, including any preparation hint. After playback, a persistent on-screen notification stays visible until the user dismisses it. If the Mac was asleep, closed or powered off at reminder time, a missed reminder whose event has not yet ended is played on wake or app start; reminders for ended events are dropped.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] At reminder time a sound plays once, followed by a spoken explanation of the event including any preparation hint.
- [ ] Sound and speech are not repeated automatically.
- [ ] After playback, an on-screen notification for the event remains visible until the user dismisses it.
- [ ] On wake or app start, a missed reminder for an event that has not ended is played once as a full reminder; reminders for ended events are not played.
