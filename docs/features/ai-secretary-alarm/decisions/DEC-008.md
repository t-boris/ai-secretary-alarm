## Context

The reminder must not be missed, but the owner must choose how it behaves while unacknowledged.

## Alternatives

1. Repeat sound and speech until the user dismisses or snoozes
2. Play once, then keep a persistent on-screen notification
3. Repeat a few times (e.g. 3 times at 1-minute intervals), then go quiet

## Decision

The reminder's sound and speech play once; afterwards a persistent on-screen notification remains until dismissed. No automatic repetition or snooze in v1.

## Reason

The owner prefers an unobtrusive reminder over a repeating alarm.

## Consequences

Simpler behaviour and no annoyance, but a reminder can be missed if the user is away from the Mac when it plays; the persistent notification is the only fallback.
