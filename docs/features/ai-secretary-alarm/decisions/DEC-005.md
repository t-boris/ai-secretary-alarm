## Context

The app runs on a Mac that may be asleep, closed or off at reminder time, risking silently lost reminders.

## Alternatives

1. Play the missed alarm immediately on wake if the event has not ended
2. Show a silent list of missed reminders
3. Duplicate reminders via Google Calendar phone notifications

## Decision

When the Mac wakes or the app starts, any missed reminder whose event has not yet ended is played immediately as a full alarm; reminders for ended events are dropped.

## Reason

Keeps the goal of reliable, hard-to-miss reminders without adding a second notification channel.

## Consequences

A reminder may fire late (e.g. after the leave time has passed); no coverage while the Mac stays off. Phone backup remains out of scope for v1.
