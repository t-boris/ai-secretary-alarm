## Context

Users may reschedule or delete assistant-created events directly in Google Calendar; reminders must not fire at stale times or for deleted events.

## Alternatives

1. A: Track assistant-created events in the calendar; reschedule moves the reminder, deletion cancels it
2. B: Changes only via voice through the assistant; calendar edits ignored
3. C: Re-check the event in the calendar just before each alarm

## Decision

The app tracks changes to its own events in Google Calendar: a time or location change updates the reminder, and deleting the event cancels it.

## Reason

Keeps calendar and alarms consistent, avoiding false or missed alarms.

## Consequences

The app must sync changes from Google Calendar for the events it created. Manually created events are still ignored (DEC-003).
