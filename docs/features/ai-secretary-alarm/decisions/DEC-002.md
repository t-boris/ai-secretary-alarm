## Context

Q-003 asked whether v1 serves only the author or other users too; this drives sign-in, Google access and privacy work.

## Alternatives

1. Personal tool for the author only
2. Product for other users (accounts, Google OAuth app verification, privacy policy)

## Decision

v1 is a personal tool for a single user (the author) connecting their own Google account. Multi-user support, public distribution and Google app verification are out of scope for v1.

## Reason

The user chose a personal tool: fastest path with minimal setup.

## Consequences

No registration or account system; one Google account connected by the owner. Supporting other users later will require adding accounts, OAuth verification and a privacy policy.
